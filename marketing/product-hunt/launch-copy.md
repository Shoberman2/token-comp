## First comment

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I built Burnt because AI usage has become part of how we work every day, but it is mostly invisible. We talk about what we ship, yet rarely about the compute behind it.

The simplest way to explain Burnt is: **it’s BeReal, but for AI tokens.**

Once a day, post a screenshot of your usage, choose the model, and share your burn. Friends see it in a feed, streaks turn it into a habit, and the leaderboard adds just enough competition.

The screenshot is the point: keep it real and bring the receipt. Burnt turns a private usage number into a social signal—what you built, which model you used, and how hard you burned that day.

This is the first version, and I’d genuinely love your feedback:

- Which AI tools or providers should Burnt support next?
- Would team or company leaderboards be useful?
- What would make you post your burn every day?

Try Burnt: https://burnt-delta.vercel.app

Thanks for checking it out. Tell me what feels fun, what feels confusing, and what would make Burnt worth coming back to tomorrow.
